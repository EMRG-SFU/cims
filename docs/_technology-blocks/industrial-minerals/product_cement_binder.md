Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Portland cement (GU, Type 10) | General-use portland cement: clinker with about 5% gypsum and up to 5% limestone (CSA A3001 GU); the binder the blends compete with. | Conventional |
| Alkali-activated binder | Geopolymer binder from slag or ash with an alkali activator, without clinker. | Alternative |
| Blended cement with supplementary cementitious materials | Replaces clinker with slag, fly ash or ground limestone. | Alternative |
| Calcined clay cement (LC3) | Clay calcined at about 800 °C replaces part of the clinker. | Alternative |
| Calcium sulphoaluminate clinker | Clinker based on ye'elimite, fired at about 1250 °C with less limestone. | Alternative |
| Carbonated binder | Calcium silicate binder cured with CO₂ rather than water. | Prospective |
| Electrochemical cement from calcium silicates | Electrolysis at ambient temperature extracts calcium from non-carbonate calcium silicates and makes a cementitious binder without a kiln, so there is no limestone process CO₂. | Prospective |
| Hydrothermal calcium hydrosilicate binder | Binder made in an autoclave and ground, with no rotary kiln (e.g. Celitement). | Prospective |
