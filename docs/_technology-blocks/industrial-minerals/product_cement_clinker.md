Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Dry rotary kiln with preheater and precalciner | Multi-stage cyclone preheater and precalciner ahead of a short rotary kiln, with a grate cooler. | Conventional |
| Legacy cement kiln (wet, semi-dry or long dry) | Wet, semi-wet, semi-dry (Lepol grate) and long dry rotary kilns: routes older than the preheater-precalciner kiln, using more than 15% more fuel per t clinker. | Conventional |
| Cement kiln efficiency measures, moderate | Combustion and sealing measures on an existing kiln line. | Efficiency variant |
| Cement kiln efficiency measures, strong | The preheater, cooler and gas-path upgrades that together take a kiln line well below its current fuel use. | Efficiency variant |
| Cement kiln with integrated calcium looping | Calciner and preheater rebuilt so the kiln concentrates its own CO₂ with a CaO sorbent loop; more fuel, power export. | Prospective |
| Cement kiln with post-combustion capture | A cement kiln whose stack gas, carrying process and combustion CO₂ together, is sent to capture. | Prospective |
| Electrified clinker kiln (plasma) | A clinker line whose kiln and calciner are heated by plasma torches and electric flow heaters instead of fuel. | Prospective |
| Electrified or indirectly heated calciner | Calcines the meal with electric heat or an indirectly heated vessel, separating process CO₂ from flue gas. | Prospective |
| Oxy-fuel kiln | Fires on oxygen and recycled flue gas, so the kiln gas is CO₂-rich. | Prospective |
| Portland clinker from calcium-silicate rock | Dissolves calcium-silicate rock instead of limestone and fires the recovered calcium to ordinary Portland clinker, co-producing a supplementary cementitious material and magnesium; no limestone process CO₂. | Prospective |
