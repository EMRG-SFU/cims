Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Intermittent ceramic kiln (shuttle or hood) | Batch-fired shuttle or hood kiln; roughly twice the energy per t of a tunnel or roller kiln. | Conventional |
| Tunnel or roller kiln | Continuous kiln firing brick, tile and refractories. | Conventional |
| Ceramic tunnel or roller kiln, strong efficiency measures | Low-mass kiln cars and fibre insulation, heat recycling between cooling and firing zones; more than 15% saving. | Efficiency variant |
| Electric kiln | Resistance-heated kiln for ceramics and technical products. | Alternative |
| Microwave-assisted ceramic kiln | A gas-fired ceramic kiln with part of the heat (about 10%) from microwaves, cutting total heat per tonne by about 40%. | Prospective |
