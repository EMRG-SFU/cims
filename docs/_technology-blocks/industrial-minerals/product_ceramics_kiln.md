| Technology | What it is | Type | TRL |

| Intermittent ceramic kiln (shuttle or hood) | Batch-fired shuttle or hood kiln; roughly twice the energy per t of a tunnel or roller kiln. | Conventional | 9 |
| Tunnel or roller kiln | Continuous kiln firing brick, tile and refractories. | Conventional | 9 |
| Ceramic tunnel or roller kiln, strong efficiency measures | Low-mass kiln cars and fibre insulation, heat recycling between cooling and firing zones; more than 15% saving. | Efficiency variant | 9 |

| Microwave-assisted ceramic kiln | A gas-fired ceramic kiln with part of the heat (about 10%) from microwaves, cutting total heat per tonne by about 40%. | Prospective | 5-7 |
