Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Ceramic ware dryer (chamber or tunnel) | Chamber or tunnel dryer removing forming water from green ceramic ware with hot air, often kiln exhaust. | Conventional |
| Direct-fired rotary or fluid-bed dryer or calciner | Hot gas in direct contact with the mineral, for drying and gypsum calcining. | Conventional |
| Electric dryer | Electric air heating, or a heat pump where the duty is below about 150 °C. | Alternative |
