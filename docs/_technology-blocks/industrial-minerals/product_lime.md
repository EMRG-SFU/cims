Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Long rotary lime kiln | Long rotary kiln burning limestone without a preheater: 6.0-9.2 GJ/t, about 18% above the preheater rotary kiln; legacy stock. | Conventional |
| Mixed-feed or annular shaft lime kiln | Vertical shaft kiln (mixed-feed, annular or single-shaft) burning lime with counter-current heat exchange. | Conventional |
| Rotary lime kiln with preheater | Rotating kiln calcining limestone, with a preheater and product cooler. | Conventional |
| Lime kiln with post-combustion capture | A lime kiln whose stack gas is sent to capture. Two thirds of the CO₂ is process CO₂ off the limestone, which no fuel switch can reach. | Prospective |
| Oxy-fuel lime kiln | Fires on oxygen and recycled gas so the kiln gas is CO₂-rich. | Prospective |
