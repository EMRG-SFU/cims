Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Diaphragm chlor-alkali cell | Diaphragm cell producing weak caustic that needs steam evaporation; legacy North American stock. | Conventional |
| Membrane chlor-alkali cell | Ion-exchange membrane cell producing chlorine, caustic soda and hydrogen. | Conventional |
| Mercury chlor-alkali cell | Mercury-cathode chlor-alkali cell; legacy stock using about 30% more electricity per t Cl2 than membrane cells. | Conventional |
| Oxygen-depolarized cathode chlor-alkali cell | Feeds oxygen to the cathode, cutting cell voltage and power use, with no hydrogen co-product. | Efficiency variant |
