Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Natural-gas reforming and methanol synthesis (whole plant) | Reformed gas converted to methanol over a copper catalyst. | Conventional |
| Coal gasification to methanol | Coal or lignite gasification and synthesis to methanol: 58-72 GJ/t against 33-37 for gas reforming, about 5 t CO₂/t. | Alternative |
| Biomass or waste gasification to methanol | Gasifies biomass or waste to syngas, then synthesizes methanol. | Prospective |
| CO₂-to-methanol | Hydrogenates captured CO₂ to methanol, directly or through reverse water-gas shift. | Prospective |
| Coal gasification to methanol with capture | A coal-to-methanol plant whose shift and acid-gas removal send the CO₂, in one concentrated stream, to capture instead of venting it. | Prospective |
