Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| No N2O abatement | The acid plant's N2O leaves untreated: the zero-cost, zero-energy option that catalytic and thermal abatement compete with. | Conventional |
| Catalytic N2O abatement | Secondary or tertiary catalyst destroying N2O in nitric and adipic acid plants. | Efficiency variant |
| Thermal N2O destruction | Adipic acid off-gas burned or thermally decomposed, destroying N2O and raising steam from the heat. | Alternative |
