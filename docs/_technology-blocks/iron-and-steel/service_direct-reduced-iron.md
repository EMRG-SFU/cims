Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Shaft-furnace direct reduction | Reduces pellets in a shaft with reformed natural gas (or a natural-gas and hydrogen blend), giving solid DRI for an EAF. The about-100% hydrogen case is shaft-furnace_hydrogen-direct-reduction. | Conventional |
| Rotary hearth furnace direct reduction | Coal-based reduction of composite pellets on a rotating hearth (Fastmet, Inmetco, ITmk3); about 20% above a gas-based shaft. | Alternative |
| Shaft-furnace direct reduction with capture | A gas-based DRI plant whose CO₂ removal unit, which already strips CO₂ from the recycled top gas, sends that nearly pure stream to capture instead of venting it. | Alternative |
| Hydrogen direct reduction | Reduces iron ore pellets (or fines, in a fluidised bed) with about 100% hydrogen instead of reformed natural gas, giving solid DRI for an EAF. Hydrogen production is not included; the whole-plant route with electrolysers is listed separately. | Prospective |
| Low-temperature iron electrowinning | Dissolves ore in an aqueous electrolyte and plates out iron near ambient temperature. | Prospective |
