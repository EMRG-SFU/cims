Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Cupola furnace | Coke cupola or gas-fired furnace melting iron and non-ferrous metal in foundries. | Conventional |
| Electric arc furnace | Melts scrap or DRI with graphite electrodes, with oxy-fuel burners and chemical energy. | Conventional |
| Gas-fired crucible melting furnace | Fuel-fired crucible furnace for non-ferrous foundry metal: 2,500-4,000 Btu/lb, about double a reverberatory. | Conventional |
| Induction melting and holding furnace | Coreless or channel induction melting, common in modern foundries. | Alternative |
| Isothermal (immersion) melting furnace | Immersed electric heaters with metal circulation; about 26% below electric resistance melting; single installation. | Alternative |
| Oxy-fuel rotary iron melting furnace | Oxy-gas rotary furnace melting cast iron: 600-800 kWh/t against 950-1,200 for the cupola, with gas rather than coke emissions. | Alternative |

:::
