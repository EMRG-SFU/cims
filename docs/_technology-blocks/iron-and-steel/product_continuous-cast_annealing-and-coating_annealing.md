Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Batch (bell) annealing | Coils annealed under a hood in a protective atmosphere over long cycles. | Conventional |
| Bell annealing in a hydrogen atmosphere | 100% hydrogen atmosphere improves heat transfer and shortens the cycle. | Efficiency variant |
| Continuous annealing line | Strip annealed in line at high speed rather than in batches. | Alternative |
| Induction strip heating | Induction heaters for annealing and galvanizing lines in place of fired furnaces. | Alternative |
