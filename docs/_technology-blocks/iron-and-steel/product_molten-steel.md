Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Basic oxygen furnace | Blows oxygen through hot metal to burn out carbon; scrap is the coolant. | Conventional |
| Electric arc furnace | Melts scrap or DRI with graphite electrodes, with oxy-fuel burners and chemical energy. | Conventional |
| Basic oxygen furnace efficiency measures, strong | Recovering the converter gas and its sensible heat instead of flaring it. | Efficiency variant |
| Electric arc furnace with scrap preheating | Ultra-high power with scrap preheating or continuous charging, cutting electricity and tap-to-tap time. | Efficiency variant |
| Electrolytic-hydrogen direct reduction with EAF (whole plant) | Electrolysers, a hydrogen shaft furnace and an electric arc furnace together, per tonne of steel. | Prospective |
