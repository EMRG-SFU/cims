Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Continuous casting | Casts liquid steel into slabs, blooms or billets in a continuous strand. | Conventional |
| Ingot casting | Casts into ingots, reheated in soaking pits before rolling. | Conventional |
| Thin-slab casting with direct rolling | Casts a thin slab and rolls it hot in line, cutting reheating and rolling passes. | Efficiency variant |
| Strip casting | Casts near-net-shape strip directly from liquid steel. | Alternative |

:::
