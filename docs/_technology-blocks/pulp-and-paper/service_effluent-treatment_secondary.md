Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Activated sludge aeration | Surface aerators or coarse-bubble diffusers holding an aeration basin at the oxygen demand of the effluent. | Conventional |
| Activated sludge efficiency measures, moderate (fine-bubble aeration) | Fine-bubble diffusers with blower control on dissolved oxygen, at a fraction of the power of surface aeration. | Efficiency variant |
| Anaerobic treatment with biogas recovery | Anaerobic reactors on high-strength streams, cutting aeration load and recovering biogas as fuel. | Alternative |
| Membrane bioreactor effluent treatment | Activated sludge with submerged membranes replacing the clarifier; about twice the energy of conventional activated sludge. | Alternative |
