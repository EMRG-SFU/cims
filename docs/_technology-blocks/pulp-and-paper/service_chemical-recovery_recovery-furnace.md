::: {.tech-block}
Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Recovery boiler | Burns concentrated black liquor, recovering smelt and raising high-pressure steam. | Conventional |
| High-solids firing and high-pressure recovery boiler | Higher liquor dry solids and steam conditions, raising power and steam yield. | Efficiency variant |
| Black liquor gasification | Gasifies liquor to syngas for fuels, chemicals or a gas turbine, in place of a recovery boiler. | Alternative |
| Recovery boiler with post-combustion capture | A recovery boiler whose stack gas is sent to capture. The CO₂ is biogenic, so this is a removal rather than an avoided emission. | Prospective |

:::
