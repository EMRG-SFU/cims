Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Continuous kraft digester | Continuous vessel cooking chips in white liquor, with heat recovery from the flash steam. | Conventional |
| Semi-chemical pulping | Milder chemical cooking for corrugating medium and specialty pulps. | Conventional |
