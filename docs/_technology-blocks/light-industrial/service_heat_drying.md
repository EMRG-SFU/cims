Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Direct-fired dryer or curing oven | Gas-fired ovens for paint curing, textile finishing and product drying. | Conventional |
| Solid-fuel dryer | A direct-fired dryer burning coal, coke, biomass or coal tars. | Conventional |
| Steam or thermal-oil lumber kiln | Batch kilns heated by hog-fuel boilers through steam or thermal oil. | Conventional |
| Continuous kiln with heat recovery | Continuous drying with zone heat recovery, at lower energy per cubic metre. | Efficiency variant |
| Direct-fired dryer efficiency measures, moderate | The direct-fired dryer or curing oven with incremental efficiency measures applied. | Efficiency variant |
| Direct-fired dryer efficiency measures, strong | The direct-fired dryer or curing oven with incremental efficiency measures applied. | Efficiency variant |
| Solid-fuel dryer efficiency measures, strong | The solid-fuel dryer with incremental efficiency measures applied. | Efficiency variant |
| Dehumidification or heat-pump kiln | Heat-pump kiln recovering latent heat from the drying air. | Alternative |
| Electric dryer | Electric air heating, or a heat pump where the duty is below about 150 °C. | Alternative |
| Infrared or ultraviolet curing | Radiant or photochemical curing in place of hot-air ovens. | Alternative |
| Superheated-steam dryer | A direct dryer using superheated steam instead of hot air as the drying medium; the evaporated water can be recovered by vapour recompression. | Alternative |
