Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Leak detection and repair | Scheduled surveys finding and fixing leaking components. | Conventional |
| No methane abatement (vent or leak) | The methane is vented or leaks as it does today: the zero-cost, zero-energy option the venting, fugitive and well-test measures compete with. | Conventional |
| Automatic shutoff and relief valves | Valves that close by themselves on an abnormal event, so the release stops without an operator: excess flow valves on service lines, flame-failure (BASO) valves on burner fuel, relief valves that reseat over a burst plate. | Alternative |
| Low-emission compressor seals and rod packing | Replaces wet seals on centrifugal compressors with dry gas seals, or fits low-emission rod packing on reciprocating compressors, cutting the methane the seals vent. | Alternative |
| Low-emission valves and connectors | Bellows-sealed and low-leak valve and connector designs cutting the leak rate of new and replaced components. | Alternative |
| Plastic insert liners in leak-prone mains | Flexible plastic liners pulled through cast iron and unprotected steel distribution mains and services, giving plastic-pipe leak rates without excavation. | Alternative |
