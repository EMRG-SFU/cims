Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Natural gas transmission pipeline | A large-diameter transmission pipeline with compressor stations every 65-160 km. Its compression comes from the compressor-drive services. | Conventional |
| Pressure-regulating valve with line heater | Throttles pipeline gas to distribution pressure at a city-gate or regulator station, with a gas-fired water-bath heater offsetting the Joule-Thomson cooling; the baseline that the let-down turboexpander competes with. | Conventional |
| Turboexpander at pressure let-down | Recovers pressure energy as power instead of throttling, with line heating. | Efficiency variant |
