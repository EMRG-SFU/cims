Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Diesel haul trucks | Diesel trucks, loaders and drills moving ore and waste. | Conventional |
| Shaft hoist | Hoists lifting ore from depth in place of ramp haulage. | Conventional |
| Dual-fuel (LNG-diesel) haul trucks | Diesel haul trucks converted to burn LNG with diesel pilot (dual fuel, roughly 30-50% gas substitution; HPDI up to 95%), cutting diesel use and CO₂ at the cost of methane slip. | Alternative |
| Trolley-assist haulage | Overhead catenary powers trucks on the ramp, cutting diesel on the loaded climb. | Alternative |
| Battery-electric haulage | Battery-electric haul trucks, loaders and underground mobile equipment. | Prospective |
| Hydrogen fuel-cell haulage | Fuel-cell trucks in demonstration at mine scale. | Prospective |

:::
