Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Gas-suspension calciner | Suspension calcination of hydrate, at lower fuel use than a rotary calciner. | Conventional |
| Electric alumina calciner | Calcines alumina hydrate with electric heating; no combustion emissions. | Prospective |
| Gas-suspension calciner with post-combustion capture | An alumina refinery whose calciner or boiler flue gas is sent to capture. | Prospective |
| Hydrogen-fired alumina calciner | Calcines alumina hydrate with hydrogen firing (air or oxy-fired), in a gas-suspension calciner retrofitted or built for hydrogen. | Prospective |
