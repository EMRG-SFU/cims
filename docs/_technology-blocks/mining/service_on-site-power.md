Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Combined-cycle gas turbine, power only | Gas turbine exhausting to a heat-recovery steam generator and condensing steam turbine; power only (combined-cycle CHP is merged into combustion-turbine_chp). | Conventional |
| Condensing steam turbine | Boiler and condensing turbine producing power only. | Conventional |
| Engine or simple-cycle turbine generator, power only | Gas-fired generator without heat recovery. | Conventional |
| Combined-cycle efficiency measures, moderate | Raising the electrical efficiency of an existing on-site set. | Efficiency variant |
| Geothermal binary-cycle power | A binary (organic Rankine) cycle generating power from moderate-temperature geothermal brine. | Alternative |
| Geothermal flash-steam power | A flash-steam cycle generating power from high-temperature geothermal brine. | Alternative |
| Solar PV with battery storage | Site-scale PV and storage displacing genset fuel. | Alternative |
| Waste-heat power (ORC or steam Rankine) | Converts exhaust or process heat to electricity. | Alternative |
| Wind turbines at site | Site wind generation, usually with storage, at remote operations. | Alternative |
| Combined-cycle gas turbine with post-combustion capture, power only | A power-only combined cycle whose HRSG exhaust is sent to capture. | Prospective |
| Oxy-fuel supercritical-CO₂ power cycle | A natural-gas cycle burning in oxygen with supercritical CO₂ as working fluid, delivering CO₂ ready for storage (Allam cycle). | Prospective |
| Small nuclear reactor for power | A small modular or micro reactor generating electricity (power-only operation). | Prospective |
