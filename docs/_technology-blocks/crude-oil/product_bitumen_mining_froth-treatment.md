Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Naphthenic froth treatment | Naphtha diluent, inclined plate settlers and centrifuges or cyclones at 70-90 °C, with naphtha recovery from the tailings. Recovers about 98% of the bitumen but leaves 1-2% water and solids and all the asphaltenes, so the product goes to an upgrader. | Conventional |
| Paraffinic froth treatment | Paraffinic solvent rejects asphaltenes, giving a cleaner bitumen product. | Alternative |
