Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Cyclic steam stimulation (CSS) | One well alternately injects steam, soaks and produces heated bitumen. The whole recovery process, its steam generation included. | Conventional |
| Steam-assisted gravity drainage (SAGD) | Paired horizontal wells: steam injected in the upper well drains heated bitumen to the lower producer. The whole recovery process, its steam generation included. | Conventional |
| Electromagnetic (radio-frequency) heating with solvent | In-situ bitumen recovery heating the reservoir with radio-frequency antennae and a solvent instead of steam. | Prospective |
| In-situ oxy-combustion steam generation | Steam generated downhole by oxy-fuel combustion, with the combustion CO₂ left in the reservoir. | Prospective |
| Solvent-assisted steam recovery | Co-injects a solvent with steam, cutting the steam-to-oil ratio. | Prospective |
| Solvent-only extraction | Dissolves bitumen with solvent and little or no steam. | Prospective |
