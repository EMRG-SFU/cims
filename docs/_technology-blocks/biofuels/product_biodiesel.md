| Technology | What it is | Type | TRL |
|:------------------------|:------------------------------------------------------|:--------------|:------|
| Alkali-catalyzed transesterification | Reacts oils or fats with methanol over a homogeneous catalyst to fatty acid methyl esters. | Conventional | 9 |
| Acid-catalyzed transesterification | Homogeneous acid catalyst tolerates free fatty acids in waste oils but needs a large methanol excess and recovery duty. | Alternative | 8-9 |
| Heterogeneous-catalyst transesterification | Solid catalyst avoids the washing and neutralization steps and gives cleaner glycerin. | Alternative | 8 |
| Supercritical-methanol transesterification | Non-catalytic transesterification in supercritical methanol (about 350 °C). | Prospective | 5-6 |
