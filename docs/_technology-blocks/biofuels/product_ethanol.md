Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Grain dry-mill fermentation and distillation | Mills, cooks and ferments starch, then distils and dehydrates the beer and dries the stillage. | Conventional |
| Grain dry-mill efficiency measures, moderate | Moderate efficiency measures on the grain dry-mill ethanol plant (molecular-sieve dehydration with heat integration). | Efficiency variant |
| Ethanol fermenter with capture of the fermentation CO₂ | An ethanol fermenter whose off-gas, about 99% CO₂, is sent to capture instead of venting it. | Alternative |
| Lignocellulosic enzymatic fermentation | Pretreats and enzymatically hydrolyzes biomass before fermentation. | Alternative |
| CO₂-to-ethanol | Ethanol synthesised from hydrogen and captured CO₂ (through methanol and DME). | Prospective |
| Syngas fermentation | Microbes convert gasified biomass or waste gas to ethanol. | Prospective |
