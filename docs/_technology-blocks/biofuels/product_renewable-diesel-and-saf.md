Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Hydrotreating of oils and fats (HEFA/HVO) | Hydrodeoxygenates and isomerizes lipids to diesel and jet range fuels. | Conventional |
| Alcohol-to-jet | Dehydrates and oligomerizes ethanol or isobutanol to jet fuel. | Prospective |
| Biomass or waste gasification to Fischer-Tropsch liquids | Gasifies biomass or waste and synthesizes liquid hydrocarbons. | Prospective |
| Biomass or waste gasification to Fischer-Tropsch liquids with capture | A gasification and Fischer-Tropsch plant (biomass, or biomass co-gasified with coal) that sends nearly all its CO₂, in one concentrated stream, to capture; the biomass share makes it a removal. | Prospective |
| CO₂-to-Fischer-Tropsch fuels | Reverse water-gas shift plus Fischer-Tropsch synthesis making jet fuel and diesel from hydrogen and captured CO₂. | Prospective |
| Hydrothermal liquefaction | Converts wet biomass to bio-crude in hot pressurized water, then upgrades it. | Prospective |
