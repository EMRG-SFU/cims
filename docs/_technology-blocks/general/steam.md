Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Back-pressure or extraction steam-turbine CHP | Boiler raises high-pressure steam; the turbine passes it to process at lower pressure. | Conventional |
| Fuel-fired boiler or thermal-oil heater | Fire-tube or water-tube boiler raising hot water or steam, or a fired heater circulating thermal oil at 200-400 °C. | Conventional |
| Gas-turbine CHP (simple or combined cycle) | Turbine exhaust raises process steam in an HRSG; in combined cycle a steam turbine sits between the HRSG and the process. | Conventional |
| Reciprocating engine CHP | Engine generator with jacket and exhaust heat recovery, at small scale. | Conventional |
| Solid-fuel boiler | Grate or fluidized-bed boiler firing solid fuels. | Conventional |
| Efficient fuel-fired boiler | The fuel-fired boiler with efficiency measures applied, including condensing operation where the return water is cold enough. | Efficiency variant |
| Waste-heat recovery unit (economizer or waste-heat boiler) | Recovers heat from flue gas or process off-gas in an economizer or waste-heat boiler and supplies it as steam or hot water. | Efficiency variant |
| Electric boiler | Resistance or electrode boiler raising hot water or steam at about 99% efficiency. | Alternative |
| Fuel-cell CHP | Solid-oxide or molten-carbonate cell producing power and usable heat. | Alternative |
| Heat transformer (heat-driven heat pump) | An absorption or chemical heat pump driven by waste heat that lifts part of it to a higher temperature (COP below 1, no compressor). | Alternative |
| Heat-charged thermal storage | Economizers and heat exchangers recovering heat from flue gas or process streams, and sensible or latent stores charged with that heat and discharged to a heat demand. | Alternative |
| Industrial heat pump or vapour recompression (up to 150 °C) | Closed-cycle heat pump or mechanical vapour recompression lifting waste or ambient heat to hot water or low-pressure steam at up to 150 °C. | Alternative |
| Line-focus solar concentrator (parabolic trough, linear Fresnel) | Parabolic-trough or linear-Fresnel collector fields raising steam or thermal oil at about 150-400 °C, often with storage. | Alternative |
| Solar thermal (non-concentrating) | Flat-plate, evacuated-tube or compound-parabolic collectors raising hot water or low-pressure steam. | Alternative |
| Very-high-temperature heat pump or vapour recompression (above 150 °C) | Closed-cycle (very-high-temperature) heat pump or steam-generating heat pump or mechanical vapour recompression delivering above 150 °C, up to about 280 °C: medium-pressure process steam or hot air. | Alternative |
| Back-pressure steam-turbine CHP with post-combustion capture | A solid-fuel boiler and back-pressure turbine CHP whose flue gas is sent to capture. Regeneration steam is drawn from the unit itself, a host-side penalty that belongs here and cuts its electricity export. | Prospective |
| Biomass gasifier CHP | Gasifier feeding an engine or turbine, with heat recovery. | Prospective |
| Boiler with post-combustion capture | A boiler whose flue gas is sent to capture. | Prospective |
| Electric thermal energy storage | Electrically charged solid or salt store discharging steam, decoupling heat from power prices. | Prospective |
| Gas-turbine or engine CHP with post-combustion capture | A gas-turbine (simple or combined cycle) or engine CHP unit whose exhaust is sent to capture. Regeneration steam drawn from the unit cuts its electricity or steam export. | Prospective |
| Geothermal heat (direct use) | Heat drawn from geothermal wells: hot water from a natural aquifer (direct use) or fluid circulated through stimulated hot rock (enhanced systems). | Prospective |
| Oxy-fuel boiler | Boiler fired on oxygen (from an air separation unit or an oxygen-conducting membrane) and recycled flue gas, so the flue gas is CO₂-rich and needs only purification and compression. | Prospective |
| Small nuclear reactor (SMR, high-temperature or micro) | Small modular, high-temperature or micro reactor supplying steam, process heat or electricity to an industrial site. | Prospective |
