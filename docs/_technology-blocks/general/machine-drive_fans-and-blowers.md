Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Gas-engine drive (compressors and pumps) | Engine-driven reciprocating compressors, common on gathering systems and at gas plants. | Conventional |
| Gas-turbine drive | Engine or turbine driving a compressor or pump directly. | Conventional |
| Induction motor, standard efficiency | IE2-IE3 squirrel-cage motor driving a pump, fan or compressor. | Conventional |
| Steam turbine drive | Back-pressure turbine driving a large compressor or pump. | Conventional |
| Gas-turbine drive efficiency measures, moderate | Driver upgrades and staging on gas-driven compression and pumping. | Efficiency variant |
| Motor system optimization (pumps, fans, compressed air) | System-level measures on motor-driven pumps, fans and compressed air: leak repair, pressure and flow optimization, sequencing, right-sizing and impeller trimming. | Efficiency variant |
| Premium-efficiency motor | IE4-IE5 motor, including permanent-magnet and synchronous-reluctance designs. | Efficiency variant |
| Variable-speed drive | Matches motor speed to load instead of throttling. | Efficiency variant |
