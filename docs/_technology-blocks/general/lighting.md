Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| High-intensity discharge high bay | Mercury vapour, metal halide or high-pressure sodium lamps in high-bay or low-bay fixtures; the incumbent high-bay lighting of 2000. | Conventional |
| T12 fluorescent with magnetic ballast | Linear T12 fluorescent lamps (40 W or 34 W 4-ft, 60-75 W 8-ft) on magnetic ballasts; the incumbent general lighting of 2000. | Conventional |
| Fluorescent high bay (T5 HO or T8 HO) | Multi-lamp T5 high-output or T8 high-output fluorescent fixtures for high-bay areas, the main replacement for HID before LED. | Efficiency variant |
| LED lighting with occupancy or daylight controls | LED lighting (general or high bay) with occupancy sensors, daylight dimming or both, which cut the hours the lights run. | Efficiency variant |
| T8 or T5 fluorescent with electronic ballast | Linear T8 or T5 fluorescent lamps on electronic ballasts for general and low-bay lighting. | Efficiency variant |
| LED high-bay luminaire | LED high-bay or low-bay luminaire replacing HID or fluorescent high-bay fixtures, without controls. | Alternative |
| LED luminaire or lamp, general lighting | LED luminaires or linear replacement lamps for general and low-bay lighting, without controls. | Alternative |
