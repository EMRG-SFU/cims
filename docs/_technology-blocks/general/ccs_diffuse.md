Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Amine post-combustion capture | Absorbs CO₂ from flue gas into an amine solvent; steam strips it back out. | Conventional |
| Advanced low-energy solvent | Second-generation solvents cutting reboiler duty against first-generation MEA. | Efficiency variant |
| Membrane or cryogenic post-combustion capture | Chills flue gas so CO₂ desublimates or condenses out. | Prospective |
| Tail-end calcium-looping capture | A carbonator captures CO₂ from flue gas on lime; an oxy-fired calciner regenerates the sorbent and releases concentrated CO₂. Burns its own fuel, unlike solvent units that draw steam. | Prospective |
