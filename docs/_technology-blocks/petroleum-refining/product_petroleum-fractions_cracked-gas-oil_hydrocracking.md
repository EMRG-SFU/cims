Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Fluid catalytic cracking | Cracks gas oil over a circulating catalyst; coke burnt off in the regenerator supplies the heat. | Conventional |
| Hydroprocessing (hydrocracking and hydrotreating) | Hydrogen-driven cracking and sulphur or nitrogen removal at pressure. | Conventional |
| Hydroprocessing with electric heating | A hydrocracker or hydrotreater whose fired feed heaters are replaced by electric heaters; hydrogen stays the process feed. | Alternative |
| Hydroprocessing with hydrogen-fired heaters | A hydrotreater whose feed heaters burn hydrogen instead of refinery or natural gas. | Alternative |
| FCC regenerator with post-combustion capture | A fluid catalytic cracker whose regenerator flue gas, the largest single stack in a refinery, is sent to capture. | Prospective |
| Oxy-fuel FCC regeneration | Burns catalyst coke in oxygen so the regenerator flue gas is CO₂-rich. | Prospective |
