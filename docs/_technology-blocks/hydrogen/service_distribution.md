Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Hydrogen pipeline transport | Compressors moving hydrogen through a dedicated pipeline to industrial users. | Conventional |
| Hydrogen liquefaction and road transport | Liquefies hydrogen for trucking where there is no pipeline; liquefaction takes about a third of the hydrogen's energy. | Alternative |
| Blending into the natural gas network | Injects hydrogen into the existing gas system at a limited volume share, delivering it to users as a blended fuel. | Prospective |
| Underground hydrogen storage (salt or lined rock cavern) | Compressed hydrogen stored in a solution-mined salt cavern or a lined rock cavern. | Prospective |

:::
