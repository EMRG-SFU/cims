Technologies

| Technology | What it is | Type |
|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|:----------------|
| Delayed coking | Thermally cracks residue to lighter products and petroleum coke. | Conventional |
| Ebullated-bed hydrocracking | Hydrogen addition in a moving catalyst bed for high conversion of residue. | Conventional |
| Fluid or flexi-coking | Fluidized thermal cracking, with the coke partly gasified for fuel gas. | Conventional |
| Partial upgrading | Field-scale treatment that meets pipeline specification without full upgrading. | Prospective |
