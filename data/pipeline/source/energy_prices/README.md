# Energy production costs and price multipliers
This data is currently fairly messy and uses quite a few assumptions. An overhaul will be needed eventually. CER team - we will be inputting our own production costs and multipliers from our other models/ECCC, so we will need to overhaul this method anyways.

Productions costs:
1) Electricity derived from CER + Brad's calculations
2) Biofuels from AFDC
2) Diesel, Gasoline, Light Fuel Oil, Heavy Fuel Oil: CER end use prices weighted average by demand
3) Natural Gas and feedstocks derived from CER benchmark prices. RNG = 1.5* Natural Gas.
4) All other fuels derived from JCIMS
